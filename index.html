5.8-GHz LC-VCO

Cadence Virtuoso · SpectreRF

Designed and simulated a complementary NMOS-PMOS LC-VCO for ISM-band operation using a differential LC resonant tank and PMOS accumulation-mode varactor. Achieved approximately 5.82 GHz resonator validation, −129.7 dBc/Hz phase noise at 1-MHz offset and ~4 mW DC power in pre-layout simulation. Physical layout is DRC/LVS clean; PEX and post-layout RF analysis are ongoing.
